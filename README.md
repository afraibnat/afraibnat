## 🐱 Hi, I'm Afra Ibnat
I'm a Computer Science and Data Science student at the University of Texas at Dallas. My work includes **web development**, **data science**, and **machine learning**. I'm interested in building tools that help people access, analyze, and understand information. 

I'm currently a **Break Through Tech AI Fellow** receiving industry mentorship and portfolio development by applying Python and machine learning to real-world classification problems.\
At my university, I am a **Web Developer** for the [Indigo Magazine](https://indigomagazinetx.com/), a student-run creative magazine, where I build responsive webpages with storytelling elements, design new features, and make frontend enhancements. I am also a **Web Editor** for [The Retrograde](https://retrogradenews.com/), an independently-run student news publication, to maintain and upload content. 

## Technical Skills

- **Frameworks**: React, Vue.js, Svelte
- **Data Science & ML**: Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, Matplotlib

## Projects
🩺 [**Kidney Transplant Waitlist Outcomes](https://github.com/Break-Through-Tech/Healthcare-1D-predicting-organ-transplant-waitlist-outcomes-and-optimizing-allocation):
Engineered and preprocessed features for non-profit MediMate Foundation through the Break Through Tech AI Fall Studio to inform organ transplant waitlist criteria, applying categorical encoding and feature scaling. `Python` `pandas` `scikit-learn` `Jupyter Notebook`

🏛️ [**McKinney Public Library Foundation - Donor & Grant Tracker**](https://github.com/UTDallasEPICS/mckinney-library-foundation):
A web application to help the McKinney Public Library Foundation board manage donors, donations, grants, and events through a centralized tracking system with role-based access and data management capabilities. `Vue.js` `Nuxt` `TypeScript` `SQLite` `Prisma` `Tailwind CSS`

📊 [**The Story of Black Businesses: Through a Pandemic Lens**](https://github.com/afraibnat/afra-bwdc-challenge):
An interactive 'scrollytelling' website with Svelte, using real-world data from the Black Wealth Data Center to narratively and visually explore United States Black business ownership and racial wealth disparities. I was selected for the Kode With Klossy x Bloomberg x BWDC Data Scrollytelling Challenge in the **Built for Everyone: Most Accessible Piece** category for clear language, inclusive design, and thoughtful data presentation. `Svelte` `JavaScript` `Data Visualization` `Scrollytelling`

📚 [**IntellectInk**](https://github.com/acm-projects/Intellect-Ink):
A mobile application designed to encourage consistent daily reading and learning through personalized, bite-sized content, including news articles, poems, books, and research based on users' interest. `React Native` `JavaScript` `Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB`
